    Standard, deep or declutter: which one do you need?

    Standard clean

    Keeps a tidy home tidy. Great every 1 to 4 weeks.

    • Dust surfaces, shelves & décor
    • Vacuum & mop all floors
    • Wipe kitchen counters & appliance fronts
    • Clean toilets, sinks, tubs & mirrors
    • Empty bins, make beds

    Best for: regular upkeep, busy weeks

    Most impact

    Deep clean

    Everything in standard, plus the grime that builds up over months.

    • Inside oven, fridge & microwave
    • Grout, tiles & limescale scrubbed
    • Baseboards, doors, switches & vents
    • Behind & under furniture
    • Cabinet fronts, blinds & fans

    Best for: first visits, Christmas, moving

    Declutter

    When the problem isn't dirt, it's stuff. We help you clear it.

    • Sort into keep, donate, toss
    • Closets, cupboards & drawers organised
    • Storage & labelling ideas
    • Haul-away arranged on request
    • Pairs perfectly with a deep clean

    Best for: spring cleaning, new chapters

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

    No. Our team arrives fully equipped with tools and products. If you prefer a particular product (or have allergies), just let us know.

    Why do you ask for photos or a video?

    Every home is different. A quick walkthrough lets us quote accurately for the scope, time and team size, so the price you get is the price you pay.

    Do I have to be home?

    Not at all. Many clients leave a key or gate code. We'll message you when we arrive and send photos when we're done.

    Which areas do you cover?

    Port of Spain, Diego Martin, Arima, Arouca, Piarco, Chaguanas, Caroni, Chase Village, Claxton Bay, San Fernando and nearby. Ask us if you're elsewhere.

    What if I'm not happy with something?

    Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and make it right, free of charge.
