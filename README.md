# Palmbreeze Home Care: concept website

A single-page, mobile-first site for **Palmbreeze Home Care**, a fictional home-cleaning company in Trinidad.

Concept build by **Portside Digital**: websites that work. Palmbreeze is not a real business.

What's on the page:
- **Squeegee hero**: a hazy film wipes off the hero photo when it comes into view
- **Services bento**: general, deep, kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, mattress/rug/upholstery, power washing and decluttering. "Add to my quote" fills in the builder
- **Scroll-lit statement**: the words light up as you scroll (the one bit of scroll animation)
- **Before/after slider**: drag to "clean" a kitchen
- **Build your clean**: clean type, room counters, extras, frequency discounts, area and date. It shows a live TT$ estimate, crew size and time, then sends the whole quote to WhatsApp already written
- **Standard vs Deep vs Declutter**: tabs on mobile, three columns on desktop
- **How it works**: send a photo or video, get a quote, we clean, walk through. The progress line fills as you scroll
- **Handyman & minor renovations** section
- **Gift certificates**: pick an occasion, amount and name, and the certificate preview updates live, then the request goes to WhatsApp
- **Home essentials** shop strip, reviews, FAQ, sticky mobile action bar (Call / Quote / WhatsApp)

Prices are sample numbers. Change `WA_NUMBER`, `PHONE` and the price tables at the top of `main.js`.

## Run it
Open `index.html`, or serve the folder (for example `npx http-server .`). There's no build step.
